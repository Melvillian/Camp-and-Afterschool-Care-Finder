How this works

  1. Pick a task above and press Open in the Sheet. It opens our shared Google Sheet at that task's row. Type your first name in claimed_by, today's date in claimed_on, and change status to claimed.
  2. Do it. The kind column says what to do. Usually that means opening the camp's page and comparing it with the draft rows in the Drafts tab.
  3. Mark each row ok (it matches the page), fixed (you changed something to match) or delete (the page does not have this camp).
  4. Finish by changing status to done.

Example. The camp's page says: "Art Camp, July 5-9, 9am to 3pm, $250." Your row should say: program_name Art Camp, start_date 2027-07-05, end_date 2027-07-09, start_time 09:00 AM, end_time 03:00 PM, cost_per_week 250.

Leave a cell blank when the page does not say. Never guess. Type dates like 2027-07-06. Not sure? Write your question in volunteer_notes and set status to needs_help; a maintainer reads these every week.
