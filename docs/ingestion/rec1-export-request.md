# Request to the three recreation departments

A short letter asking for a spreadsheet of summer 2027 camp sessions. Send one copy to each department below, with the department's name filled in. Replace everything in square brackets before sending. Do not add claims about the site that are not true.

Who sends it is not decided yet. If no export has arrived by early January, the volunteers will type the sessions in by hand (see `docs/ingestion/runbook.md`, part 8).

## Send to

- Burlington Parks, Recreation & Waterfront
- South Burlington Recreation & Parks
- Colchester Parks & Recreation

Winooski Recreation & Park also uses the same registration system (Rec1) and can be added to this list. The letter below is not addressed to it.

## Letter

Subject: Request for a spreadsheet of summer 2027 camp sessions

Dear [department name] staff,

I am a volunteer with the Camp & Afterschool Care Finder, a free website that helps Vermont families find summer camps and afterschool programs. It is free and run by volunteers. The site is at https://kefortney.github.io/Camp-and-Afterschool-Care-Finder/ and its code is open at https://github.com/kefortney/Camp-and-Afterschool-Care-Finder.

Your summer camps are on the site. Families filter by grade, town, cost, subject and week, and each listing links back to your registration page, so families sign up with you.

I am writing to ask for help keeping your listings correct for summer 2027. Would it be possible to send us a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel is fine) of your summer 2027 camp sessions, once they are set? For each session, we would need:

- the name of the camp or session
- start and end dates
- start and end times
- ages or grades
- price
- location
- the registration link

A one-time export from your registration system would be plenty. Anything you already have in a spreadsheet works. We would use it only to list your camps, with a link back to you.

Without it, volunteers will read your registration pages and retype each session by hand. We do that carefully and a person checks every row, but retyping is slow, and a typo could send a family to the wrong dates or price. Your own file would be quicker for us and more accurate for them.

If it would help, we can also send you what we show for your camps now so you can correct it. And if there is someone else I should ask, or a better way to receive this, please tell me. If you are not able to share this, I understand, and I will not ask again.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,

[sender's name]
[contact line: email address and phone number]
